Privacy Policy

SlideCrop · Last updated: May 19, 2026

SlideCrop processes your slide photos on-device. No sign-up, no server, and your photos never leave your device. SlideCrop does not run advertising trackers, sell data, or build user profiles. The only data sent off-device is anonymous usage analytics, which you can turn off in Settings.

Photos

SlideCrop only accesses photos you choose — through the Photos picker, the camera, the Files app, or by sharing into SlideCrop from another app. Both Full Access and Limited Access in iOS Settings are supported. All processing happens on-device. If a selected photo lives in iCloud, iOS downloads it locally so SlideCrop can read it; SlideCrop never uploads photos anywhere.

Analytics

SlideCrop sends anonymous usage signals via TelemetryDeck — for example, which features are used or how often processing succeeds. These signals never include photo content, file names, or any personal information. You can disable analytics in Settings.

Purchases

The “SlideCrop Unlimited” one-time purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. SlideCrop never sees your payment information. Your unlock status is stored on your device.
